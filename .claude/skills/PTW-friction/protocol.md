# Friction Protocol

*Every workshop participant operates under these rules. They exist because multi-perspective simulations fail in a known way: five lenses on the same document produce consensus that looks like validation, invented details flow downstream as evidence, and the biggest assumption glides through unchallenged — exactly like real strategy meetings.*

## Rules for every participant

1. **You are here to get the analysis right, not to make the workshop smooth.** Being agreeable is a failure state; so is performing your role. Your function lives with the consequences.

2. **Tag every claim** as `[EVIDENCE: source]` or `[ASSUMPTION]`. If you cannot name the source, the tag is `[ASSUMPTION]`. No third category. "Everyone knows" is an assumption.

3. **You only know your private dossier plus the shared brief.** Do not read other participants' dossiers. Do not invent facts, numbers, quotes, customers, or events. You may reason beyond your dossier, but reasoning is tagged `[ASSUMPTION]`.

4. **You may not agree with a colleague unless you name the specific evidence that moved you.** "Fair point" without evidence is banned.

5. **Attack the weakest load-bearing claim you can see** — the claim that, if false, most changes the strategy, and that has the least evidence behind it. Attacking is your job. If your own claim is the weakest, say so before someone else does.

6. **State what would change your mind.** A position with no falsification condition is an opinion.

7. **Concessions are honorable; compromises are not.** A disagreement is resolved ONLY by (a) evidence that moves one side, or (b) conversion into a testable condition. Never by a third position neither side held. Unresolved disagreements are preserved verbatim in the Collision Log — they are findings, not failures.

## Rules for the facilitator and synthesis agents

8. **Fiction firewall.** Remember-the-Future outputs are hypothesis generators. Every future file carries a `[FICTION]` header. Nothing from a future file may ever appear as evidence, be quoted as a finding, justify a confidence upgrade, or introduce an Option/choice as if participants had proposed it. The ONLY legal export from fiction is a hypothesis converted into an explicitly scored assumption with `origin: fiction`.

9. **Provenance survives synthesis.** A claim enters a synthesis document only with its tag intact. If two sources conflict, the synthesis records the conflict; it does not average it.

10. **Ranking is arithmetic, not judgment.** Assumption quadrants and rankings are computed by `scripts/rank_assumptions.py`, never by an agent's prose. No silent drops: everything below threshold is listed as excluded.

11. **The workshop does not end in a verdict.** It ends in: the draft, the Collision Log, the evidence gaps, the ranked assumption map, and experiments whose falsification criteria are written before anyone is invested in the results. Next steps are gated on evidence events (things customers and the world do), not on internal milestones.
